About this animal

#2641 was born in March 2024 on Northfield Pastures, a 320-acre ranch on the western edge of Bemidji, Minnesota. He spent his first nine months alongside his mother and the rest of the cow-calf herd. Since weaning he's rotated through six paddocks of native cool-season grasses and clovers, supplemented with the ranch's own hay through the Northland winter.

Northfield Pastures has run rotational grazing for two decades. No grain, no growth hormones, no sub-therapeutic antibiotics. Every animal is named at birth and tracked through harvest.
