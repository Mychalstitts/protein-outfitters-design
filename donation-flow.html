If a program launched, a donor might receive documentation of what was given. Any tax outcome is between the donor and their CPA.

About the deduction: For raised livestock, the IRS generally allows a deduction at your tax basis (your accumulated production costs). For most cash-basis farmers those costs were already deducted as expenses, so the deduction is small — but you also avoid recognizing income on a sale you didn't make. Some donations of "wholesome food" qualify for up to 2× basis. Talk to your CPA for your specific situation. Watch for state-level credits in Minnesota.
