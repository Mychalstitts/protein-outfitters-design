PS1 Mobile Harvest Unit

USDA-inspectable harvest on a trailer.

The PS1 is a complete, federally-inspectable kill floor in a 36-foot insulated trailer. Designed by a 4th-generation meat cutter who needed something that solved real-world problems: capacity bottlenecks, animal welfare, transport costs, and compliance complexity.

It includes a knock pen, hoist, dressing rail, evisceration table, USDA inspector workstation, hot-water sanitation, a cooler bay, and CIP-clean drainage. Drop it on a level pad. Hook up 200-amp three-phase power and a 15-gpm water supply. You're processing within 24 hours of arrival.
