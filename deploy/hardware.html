Mobile Harvest Unit (MHU)

USDA-compliant harvest on a 36-foot trailer.

The MHU is a complete, USDA-compliant harvest unit on a 36-foot trailer. Pulled to your farm or ranch, started up with the on-board 20 kW generator, and processing within hours. 15 years and four design generations of refinement.

Smooth-side, coved-edge interior built to clean fast and prevent bacterial growth. The trailer is divided into knock-and-bleed and harvest sections, with installed equipment required for federal-inspection operation. ~15 head of beef per trip.
