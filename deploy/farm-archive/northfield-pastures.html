About the ranch

Two decades of grass and patience.

Northfield Pastures sits on 320 acres of native cool-season grassland on the western edge of Bemidji, Minnesota. The Hansen family has run rotational grazing here since 2004 — moving the herd through six paddocks of bluegrass, timothy, and white clover, then stockpiling the rest for Northland winters.

Every animal is named at birth. No grain. No growth hormones. No sub-therapeutic antibiotics. The cow-calf pairs stay together until natural weaning at nine months, after which the steers and heifers spend another year and a half on pasture before harvest.

The ranch is a USDA Process Verified operation and an American Grassfed Association member. Our partner processor is Plant 04 in Bemidji, twelve miles down the road.
