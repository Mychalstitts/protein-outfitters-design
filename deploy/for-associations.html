Member onboarding one-pager

Protein Outfitters is a live nationwide marketplace. Farms list named animals by the share. Buyers pay a deposit. Plants claim their profile for booked drop-offs. Send one link — we handle the rest.

What your members get

  • Producers — free listing, share inventory, public ranch profile, Stripe Connect payouts after drop-off check-in
  • Processors — claim existing map rows (FSIS / association imports), set fees & capacity, 6-digit dock check-in
  • Buyers — reserve quarter / half / whole, cut sheet, local plant pickup
