USDA AMS Local Food Directory.

Pull farms that already self-identify as direct-to-consumer livestock producers from the USDA Agricultural Marketing Service. We filter for beef, pork, lamb, goat, bison, venison, poultry, and egg producers and stage them in discovered_partners. After import, send invites from the admin overview.

1 Open a USDA Local Food directory

Pick whichever directory has the producers you want first — most livestock farms list under CSA or On-Farm Markets:

CSA Directory~7,500 farms On-Farm Markets~1,500 farms Farmers Markets~9,000 markets Food Hubs~400 hubs

2 Export to CSV

On any directory's results page, click the Export to CSV button at the top right of the listings table. (You don't need to filter first — we filter on our side.) Or apply a "Products: Meat" filter on the portal first to keep the file smaller.

3 Upload CSV here

Drag-drop the CSV or paste the raw text. We'll filter for listings whose Products / Categories / Name mention beef, pork, lamb, goat, bison, venison, poultry, or eggs and insert into the staging table.
