Why this matters

Customer cut sheets read this config. If you don't process pork, "Bacon" never appears. If you don't grind 90/10, no one can request it. Your prices become the prices buyers see — no surprises.

Tip: Even if you're a small plant, fill this out completely. Buyers see your config when picking a processor — a complete listing converts better than a half-finished one.
