How it works

Three taps. Then a full freezer.

No phone tag. No paper cut sheets. No mystery meat. Everything happens on one page on your phone.

01 · Reserve

Pick your share.

Quarter, half, or whole — from a named animal on a verified farm. Deposit locks your place; free cancel up to 21 days before harvest.

02 · Tailor

Tell the plant how to cut it.

Steaks vs. roasts. Patty thickness. Bones for stock. Five minutes in your account — the processor gets a clean cut sheet.

03 · Pick up

Walk in. Scan. Walk out.

We text you when it’s ready. Bring a cooler to the plant. Your freezer is full that afternoon.
